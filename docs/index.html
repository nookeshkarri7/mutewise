Mutewise

Hear the alerts that matter. Mute the rest.

Mutewise is a free notification manager for Android. Write rules in plain sentences, test them on your recent notifications, and let them run in the background.

Get it on Google Play

What you can do with it

  • Give a busy group chat one buzz per burst with Cooldown.
  • Swipe away promos for you, or snooze shopping apps until 7 pm.
  • Ring an alarm, even on silent, when a doorbell camera sees a person.
  • Hide what private messages say, give one contact its own vibration, auto-reply or tap "Mark as read".
  • See every notification in History and restore anything a rule removed.
  • Check notification access and battery settings on the Health screen, so rules keep working.

Notifications are read on your phone and never leave it. There is no account. Mutewise is free and supported by ads, with no banner ads.
